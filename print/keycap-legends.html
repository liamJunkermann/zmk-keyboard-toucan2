What the marks mean

A ⇧ ⇥ ⌫ — The base layer — what the key sends on its own.

1 … 0 — Top-left corner: hold SYM (left middle thumb) and press the key.

SYM / NAV — Hold that thumb for the layer; tap it for the glyph above the word.

⌃ ⌥ — Ctrl and Opt, printed on the edge facing the key you press it with — ⌃ between S·D and K·L, ⌥ between D·F and J·K.

▬ — The bar runs under S·D·F and J·K·L: press all three together for ⌃⌥.

50 mm — if this bar measures anything else, the printer scaled the page and the decals will not fit.
